always 3 types of operations so if we have the operation of a group a^n -- Power(a,n) we always have 3 implementations:
- scalar ; that we have a^n and get 1 result
- vectorial with 1 power ; power(a_1,...a_n),m and it returns a vector of a_1^m , ... , a_n^m
- double vectorial ; power(a_1,...a_n),m_1 , .... , m_n and returns  a_1^m_1 , ... , a_n^m_n (has to make sure that it's the same length)
